Delete your Saluxly account

You can delete your Saluxly account at any time, from the app or by email. Deletion is permanent.

In the app

  1. Open Saluxly and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account, at the bottom.
  3. Confirm.

If you already uninstalled the app

Email contact@saluxly.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We confirm the request by replying to that address, then delete the account.

What is deleted, and when

Your account and its data are deleted from our live systems immediately; copies in our backups expire within 30 days.

That includes your email address, your profile, the articles you opened and saved, your daily counts and your subscription status.

Notes, highlights and offline copies of articles live only on your device; they go when you delete the app.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it first in your store account settings.

Details are in section 8 of the Privacy policy.
