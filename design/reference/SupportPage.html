Common questions

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Saluxly. Cancelling keeps your access until the end of the period you paid for.

Restore a purchase on a new device

Sign in with the same account, open Profile in the app and tap Restore purchases.

An article will not open

Papers marked with an outlined page open at PubMed Central or the journal’s website, which can be slow or show its own security check. Try again in a minute, or open the PubMed record from the article details.

Change your language, profession or specialties

All three live in Profile. Changes apply to your next feed.

Delete your account

Deleting your account removes your profile, your reading history and your saved articles. Subscriptions are cancelled separately in your App Store settings.

  1. Open Saluxly and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account, at the bottom.
  3. Confirm. Your data is erased within 30 days.

Already uninstalled the app? Email contact@saluxly.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”.
