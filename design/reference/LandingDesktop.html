Aspirin for the prevention of preterm preeclampsia: a systematic review and meta-analysis

M. Rivera, A. Okafor, L. Chen, S. Duarte
BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth, 2025, 18 pages, CC BY
Pre-eclampsiaAspirinPrevention
Abstract

Preterm preeclampsia remains a leading cause of maternal and perinatal morbidity. We searched MEDLINE, Embase and CENTRAL for randomized trials of low-dose aspirin started before 20 weeks in women at increased risk. Eighteen trials met the criteria. Low-dose aspirin reduced preterm preeclampsia when started before 16 weeks, with no increase in major bleeding; the benefit was smaller after 16 weeks.
