Saluxly is an app for finding and reading published medical review articles. This policy explains what personal information Saluxly, Inc. collects when you use the Saluxly apps and saluxly.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. At a glance

  • Who is responsible. Saluxly, Inc., a Delaware corporation. You can reach us at contact@saluxly.com.
  • What we collect. Your email address and the profile you choose (profession, specialties and training level). Your activity in the app, such as the articles you open and save and your searches. Your subscription status, and basic technical data from your device.
  • Why we collect it. To run your account, choose the articles in your feed, apply the free plan's daily limits, manage subscriptions, send the weekly notification if you turn it on, and keep the service secure.
  • What we do not do. We do not show ads, sell your information, share it for advertising or use it to train AI models. Saluxly contains no advertising or cross-app tracking tools.
  • Who can see what you read. No other user can. We keep only combined counts, such as how often an article is opened by people in the same specialty, that are not linked to anyone.
  • Search and AI. The text of each new search is sent to our AI provider so it can be understood in any language. It is sent without your name, email or account identifier. If the same text has been searched before, we reuse the stored interpretation instead.
  • What stays on your device. Your notes, highlights and offline copies of articles are stored on your device, not on our servers.
  • Payments. Apple or Google processes your payment, including any free trial. We never receive your card details.
  • Your control. You can change your profile at any time and delete your account in the app under Profile › Delete account.
  • Who Saluxly is for. Healthcare professionals and students aged 18 or older. Saluxly is not designed to hold patient information, so never enter any.

In this policy, "personal information" means information that identifies you or can reasonably be linked to you. "Saluxly", "we" and "us" mean Saluxly, Inc.

5. Personalization, statistics and AI

Your feed is chosen automatically. Saluxly selects and orders articles using your profile, the articles you have already opened, and each article's characteristics (such as topic, relevance, journal and date). You can change your profession, specialties and feed mix at any time under Profile, and your feed changes with them.

Combined statistics are not linked to you. We count how often each article is opened, grouped by profession, specialty and week. The counts do not record who did what, and no one, including other users, can see your reading activity through them. We do not publish statistics about groups small enough to single anyone out. Because the counts are not linked to you, they cannot be traced back to you or undone.

Search uses an AI model. To understand searches in any language, including misspellings, we send the text of your search to Anthropic, our AI provider, unless our cache already holds an interpretation of the same text. We send only the text, with no name, email address or account identifier. Anthropic processes it on our behalf, is contractually barred from training its models on it, and normally deletes it within 30 days; it may keep a search longer only if its automated safety systems flag it as a possible misuse of its service or the law requires it. If you prefer not to send search text to an AI provider, do not use search; the rest of Saluxly works without it. We keep a cache of search texts and their interpretations, with no link to any account, so that repeated searches are faster. Type only what you want to search for: anything you type in the search box is sent to our AI provider and kept in our cache, so do not include your own or anyone else's personal details.

Articles are organized with AI, without your data. We use AI models to label articles by specialty, relevance and topic, and to translate their titles. This uses only information about the articles, never information about you. The Aa sheet shows each article's details and its published abstract; Saluxly does not generate summaries.

"Ask AI" sends an article to an app you choose. When you tap AI on an article, your device's share menu opens and you choose an app, such as an AI assistant, to receive the article's PDF. Saluxly itself sends nothing to those companies. The app you choose handles what you share under its own privacy policy.

We do not train AI on your information. We do not use your personal information to train AI models. Our AI provider is contractually barred from training its models on your search text, and we give no provider permission to use your information for that purpose.

7. Sites and apps you choose to open

Saluxly links to services run by others. When you open one, it collects information directly from you under its own privacy policy, not ours.

  • Journal sites, PubMed and PubMed Central. Articles that are not in our catalog open in a browser view provided by your device, on PubMed Central or on the journal's site. The site you visit can see your IP address and may set cookies. To make these sites open faster, the app may open a network connection to up to three sites in advance while their links are in the list you are viewing. That connection carries nothing about you beyond your IP address. No page or file is loaded until you tap. When you open one of these articles, the app tells our server that it was opened; the server only adds it to the combined counts described in Section 5 and keeps no record of which articles you opened on other sites. We learn nothing about what you do on that site.
  • AI apps and other apps you share with. The app you choose receives the article you share.
  • App stores. Apple and Google process your purchases and give us the subscription information described in Section 3.

We are not responsible for how these services handle your information. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

What happens when you delete your account

When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, opened and saved articles, and subscription status from our live systems immediately. Copies in backups expire within 30 days. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to end the connection between your Apple account and Saluxly.

After deletion we keep only the following. Messages you sent us, for the period in the table. Server access records (IP address, date and time) for six months, as the table says. Copies in backups and other logs until they expire, within 30 days. The search cache and the combined statistics, which are not linked to any account. Anything the law requires us to keep. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep the purchase records they need under their own policies.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google. Cancel it first in your store account settings.

9. Security and where your information is processed

We protect your information with safeguards suited to a service of our size. These include encrypting data in transit, limiting who can access our systems, and avoiding passwords: you sign in through Apple, Google or a one-time code.

No online service can guarantee perfect security. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Saluxly, Inc. is a Delaware corporation, and its team works from Mexico. Our servers are located in the United States. Your information is processed in the United States, accessed by our team from Mexico, and handled by our providers in the countries where they operate. Data protection laws in those countries may differ from the laws where you live. Where the law requires safeguards for international transfers, we rely on recognized mechanisms such as standard contractual clauses (contract terms approved by regulators for sending data abroad) with our providers.

10. Your choices and rights

Choices in the app

  • Profile and feed. Change your profession, specialties, training level, feed mix and language under Profile.
  • Notifications. Turn them on or off in the app under Profile, or in your device settings, at any time.
  • Saved articles, notes and highlights. Remove them whenever you like.
  • Sign out. Signing out removes your session from the device.
  • Delete your account. Go to Profile › Delete account. If you have already uninstalled the app, follow the steps at saluxly.com/support.html#delete or write to contact@saluxly.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know whether we hold personal information about you, and get a copy of it;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • object to or restrict certain uses, including uses based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent you have given, such as for notifications;
  • complain to the data protection authority where you live.

We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

How to make a request

Write to contact@saluxly.com, and tell us the email address on your account and what you would like. To make sure the request is yours, we send a confirmation message to the account's address. If you signed in with Apple and hid your email address, that message reaches you through Apple's relay. We ask for other proof of identity only if we have no other way to verify a request.

We answer within the time your local law sets, normally within 30 days. Requests are free. If we cannot do what you ask, we explain why. You can ask us to reconsider by replying to our answer, and you can always contact your local authority.

Someone you authorize in writing may make a request for you.

11. Additional information for some regions

United States

Saluxly is below the size thresholds of most U.S. state privacy laws, but we apply the following to everyone in the United States.

  • No sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (advertising based on your activity across other companies' apps and sites). We never have.
  • Categories we collect. In the terms used by California law, we collect: identifiers (email address, account identifiers, IP address and the device identifier our subscription provider receives); commercial information (subscription records); internet or network activity (your activity in the app); and professional information (profession and specialties). Sections 3, 4 and 6 describe the sources, purposes and recipients.
  • Sensitive personal information. We do not collect the kinds of sensitive personal information that California law lets you restrict, so there is nothing to limit.
  • Your rights. Depending on your state, you can ask to know, access, correct, delete and receive a portable copy of your personal information. Section 10 explains how. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.
  • Appeals. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision. If we decline your appeal, you can contact your state attorney general.
  • Browser signals. We do not respond to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals (browser settings that ask sites not to sell or share your data) because we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we treat every user as opted out of sale and sharing.
  • California "Shine the Light". We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

  • Controller and legal bases. Saluxly, Inc. is the controller of your personal data. Our legal bases are listed in Section 4.
  • Your rights. You have the rights described in Section 10 under the GDPR and equivalent laws. You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Complaints. You can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live or work. In the United Kingdom, this is the Information Commissioner's Office.
  • International transfers. Your data is processed in the United States and accessed from Mexico. Neither country is covered by an EU or UK adequacy decision for Saluxly's own processing. Where a transfer safeguard is required, we use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (for the UK, the ICO's international data transfer addendum) with our providers, or another mechanism the law allows. Write to contact@saluxly.com for a copy of the clauses we rely on.
