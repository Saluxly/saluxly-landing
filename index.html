Built for how doctors read.

Short on time and long on standards. Saluxly brings you the paper, not a link farm.

Chosen for you, not for advertisers.

Every paper in your feed is there because of your specialties and what you read, never because someone paid for the spot. No ads, no sponsors, no pharma.

The original paper, as the journal published it.

Open-license papers (CC BY and CC0) open inside Saluxly in a native reader. Highlight, take notes and keep them offline. Everything else opens at PubMed Central or the journal’s site, clearly marked.

Understand it in two minutes.

Aa gives you a plain-language summary built from the paper itself. Ask AI answers your questions about what you are reading. Both can make mistakes, so the paper is always one tap away.

Search that speaks medicine.

Type in English, Spanish or Portuguese, with abbreviations or typos. Saluxly maps your words to medical subject headings and returns the reviews that are about your topic, not the ones that merely mention it.

Questions, answered.

Is Saluxly a textbook or a clinical reference?

No. Saluxly is a reading feed of review articles from the medical literature. It helps you keep up with what is being published in your field; it does not replace a clinical reference, a guideline or your own judgment.

Where do the articles come from?

From PubMed, the database of the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Saluxly keeps review articles, systematic reviews, meta-analyses and guidelines from the last five years, published in journals that pass its quality bar.

Can I read the full paper?

Yes. Papers with an open license (CC BY or CC0) open inside the app as the original PDF. Other papers open at PubMed Central or on the journal’s website in a browser view, where many are free to read.

Is it legal to read the PDFs in the app?

Yes. Saluxly only hosts papers whose license allows it, credits every paper to its authors and journal, and links the rest to their source instead of copying them.

Which languages does it work in?

The app runs in English, Spanish and Portuguese. Articles are in English, with titles translated automatically. You can search in any of the three languages.

Do I need a hospital or university login?

No. Everything in Saluxly is either openly licensed or free to read at its source. You only need an email address.

Which devices can I use?

iPhone and iPad first, including an iPad layout and a home-screen widget. Android follows, and a web version is planned.

What do you do with my data?

Your email signs you in. What you read and save personalizes your feed. Saluxly shows no ads, uses no advertising trackers and does not sell your data. You can delete your account from the app at any time.
